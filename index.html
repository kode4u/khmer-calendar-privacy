Summary

Lunar Khmer Calendar does not ask you to create an account and Kode4U does not collect or sell your personal information. Your settings, saved dates and reminders stay on your device. A few third-party services are used to show weather, exchange rates, ads and notifications, as described below.

Information stored on your device

  • App settings (language, dark mode, widget design, reminder times).
  • "My dates" you add (names and lunar dates) and scheduled reminders.
  • Data for the home-screen widgets.

This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Location (weather)

If you allow location access, the app sends your approximate location to OpenWeatherMap to get the current weather. It is used only for that request. Kode4U does not store it. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA/UK), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy settings. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings.

Notifications

  • Reminders (Sila days, holidays, your dates) are scheduled on your device and do not send any data.
  • News and announcements use Firebase Cloud Messaging, which assigns your device a push token. You can turn this off in Settings → Notifications from the app; the token is then deleted.

Exchange rates and more apps

The app downloads exchange rates and a list of other Kode4U apps from our server (apps.kode4u.tech). Like any web server, it may keep standard access logs (such as IP address and time) for security and is not used to identify you.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

  • Delete saved dates inside the app (My dates → swipe to delete), or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Turn off "Notifications from the app" to delete the push token.
  • Reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

Kode4U keeps no account or personal profile about you, so there is nothing else to delete.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Questions: Kode4U on Facebook.
